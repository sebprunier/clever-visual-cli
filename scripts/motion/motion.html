clever-visual-cli

The Clever Cloud CLI, visually.

a visual interface of clever-tools · with a few extras

01 · The idea

The CLI, laid out on a screen.

Every screen stands for a clever command, with the real ids, one click to copy. Use it, and you learn clever-tools along the way.

Not a new Console: a static page, between your browser and the Clever Cloud APIs.

02 · Filter & group

A whole organisation, sliced your way.

Expressions to filter, tags to group, up to three levels.

filter
group by

03 · Views

Save it as a view.

Filter, grouping and sort, one click away. Kept per organisation, and synced across your browsers through a configuration provider of your own.

04 · Costs

What does it cost? Per project, per env.

Yesterday, the last 30 days, the latest invoice or the current configuration, summed by group.

CSVJSONMarkdown

05 · Act on a selection

Tick, then act on all of them.

Restart or stop several applications, add or rename tags, open their logs together.

$ clever restart$ clever stop

06 · Logs of several apps

Several apps, one timeline.

Side by side in a grid, or merged into one stream, every line coloured by its application.

When something breaks, the API, the gateway and the worker tell the same story, line after line.

07 · One application

Every tab, one command.

Overview, activity, domains, variables, dependencies and logs: each tab names the clever command it stands for.

08 · Application + access logs

See the request behind the error.

Application and access logs, merged in time: analysis and debugging, in one place.

An error in the application logs: the payment provider timed out…

…and the request behind it: POST /api/payments, a 502 after 5 s, then a 404 on order 4812.

09 · At a glance

Is everything OK?

The Clever Cloud status, and what needs you: failed deployments, outdated databases, operator updates.

10 · Your token, your browser

No backend. Nothing to install.

Sign in with an API token, encrypted in your browser or kept for the tab only. Several accounts, one click to switch.
